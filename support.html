If you already subscribed or bought Lifetime Access, open the paywall or go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the Microsoft Store with the same Microsoft account used for the purchase.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Visit account.microsoft.com/services, sign in, and choose TExES PPR Exam Prep 2026 under Services & subscriptions.

Keyboard shortcuts

In a quiz: A–E or 1–5 to answer (select-all questions: pick every answer, then Enter to check), Enter for next, ← for the previous question, Esc to leave. In flashcards: Space to flip, 1 review again, 2 got it.
